Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Word Me! ("we", "our", or "the app") is an English learning application designed to help users improve their vocabulary through words, translations, pronunciation, examples, and practice exercises.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Information We Collect

Word Me! does not require users to create an account.

The app may process:

  • Learning progress and preferences stored on your device
  • Words and sentences entered by users for practice exercises
  • App usage information needed for functionality

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

How We Use Information

  • Provide vocabulary learning features
  • Save learning progress
  • Provide translation and pronunciation features
  • Improve app functionality

Data Storage

Word Me! stores learning data locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Translation and Language Services

Word Me! uses language translation technology to provide translation features.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party libraries or services required for app functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Word Me! does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
